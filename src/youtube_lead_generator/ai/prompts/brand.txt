</role>

<task>

</task>

<rules>
- Only extract brands/products that are explicitly mentioned or clearly visible in the data. Do not guess brands that aren't evidenced.
- Differentiate between:
  - "brands": Companies and brand names (e.g., "Nike", "Tebex", "FiveM")
  - "games": Video games referenced (e.g., "GTA V", "Minecraft")
  - "software": Tools, frameworks, libraries, and applications (e.g., "ox_lib", "Visual Studio Code")
  - "hardware": Physical devices mentioned (e.g., "RTX 4090", "Stream Deck"). Return an empty list if none are found — do NOT return "null" as a string.
  - "technologies": Broader tech categories (e.g., "Lua Scripting", "Discord Webhooks")
  - "creators": Other YouTube creators mentioned or collaborated with
- For "confidence", return 0-100. High confidence when brands are explicitly named; lower when inferred from context.
</rules>
